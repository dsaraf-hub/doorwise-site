How it works

No beacons. No floor plans. No special hardware. A phone's own sensors are enough.

01 · MAP

One walk per building

A mapper walks the corridors tapping doors while the phone's sensors capture the structure: steps, turns, lifts, floors. Because residential towers repeat floors, one surveyed corridor makes every floor routable: a 240-unit tower is mapped in minutes.

02 · GUIDE

Turn-by-turn to the flat

Riders get simple indoor directions (“Gate 2, service lift, floor 11, fourth door on the right”) with live position from sensor fusion. Works on budget Android phones, including those without barometers or gyroscopes.

03 · SEE

Live indoor visibility

Operations teams watch the rider inside the building in real time, from lobby to lift to corridor to door, closing the blind spot between “reached location” and “delivered.”

Common questions

Does it work on budget Android phones?

Yes. It's designed for India's rider fleet first. Guidance degrades gracefully on phones without a barometer or gyroscope: we detect lifts from the accelerometer alone and fall back to step-and-compass guidance, so even entry-level devices get useful directions.

Do you need beacons, WiFi infrastructure, or floor plans?

No. One person walks the building once with a normal phone. The phone's own sensors (accelerometer, gyroscope, barometer, compass) capture the structure. Nothing is installed in the building.

How long does mapping a building take?

About 15 minutes for a 22-floor, 240-unit tower. Residential towers repeat their floor layout, so surveying one corridor makes every floor routable.

How do riders get the directions?

During pilots, through our app. At scale, guidance embeds into the platform's existing rider app, and the rider sees simple instructions ("Gate 2, service lift, floor 11, fourth door on the right") inside the flow they already use.

What about resident privacy?

We map corridors, lifts, and door numbers: the same information printed on the building's own signage. No cameras are required and nothing inside any home is recorded.
